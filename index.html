The Algeria Squad

Algeria arrived at the 2026 FIFA World Cup with a blend of experienced leaders and emerging talent, marking their return to football's biggest stage after a 12-year absence. Guided by coach Vladimir Petković, the Fennecs combined the creativity of captain Riyad Mahrez with the pace of Amine Gouiri and Mohamed Amoura, while veterans such as Aïssa Mandi, Ramy Bensebaïni, and Nabil Bentaleb provided leadership and stability. Young talents including Ibrahim Maza, Fares Chaïbi, and Jaouen Hadjam added energy and technical quality, giving Algeria a balanced squad capable of competing with the world's best. Defensively organised and dangerous on the counterattack, the team relied on a strong collective spirit rather than individual stars alone. Every player in the squad had a role to play, from experienced campaigners to promising newcomers, as Algeria aimed to make a lasting impression and continue the proud tradition of the Desert Foxes on the global stage.
