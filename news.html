DEFEAT!!!

The final whistle in Vancouver echoed like a sudden death knell. Algeria’s 2026 World Cup dream died in a quiet, agonizing 2-0 defeat to Switzerland, built on cruel, self-inflicted wounds. A catastrophic defensive lapse allowed Breel Embolo to strike first, silencing the Algerian support. Hope lingered until Dan Ndoye doubled the Swiss lead, punishing another costly hesitation. The usually resilient Fennecs looked heavy-legged, the moment's immense weight dragging them down. Adding to the profound tragedy, Captain Riyad Mahrez and Aïssa Mandi both announced their retirements. It was a bitterly poetic end for Algeria's fading icons on a desolate Canadian evening.
